FAQs

Questions, answered

What is Collegekhoj?

Collegekhoj is being developed as a course discovery and guidance platform to help learners explore online degree options and understand the admission process.

Are online degrees valid?

Recognition and eligibility depend on the specific university, programme and admission session. Before enrolling, verify that the exact programme is entitled/recognised for online delivery with the university and relevant official regulator.

What are the fees and eligibility criteria?

Fees, eligibility, specialisations and duration vary by programme and intake. Please verify the latest details on the university’s official website before applying.

Does Collegekhoj guarantee admission or a job?

No. Admission decisions are made by the university, and employment outcomes depend on many factors. Collegekhoj provides information and guidance only.

How do I get course guidance?

Submit the form below with your contact details and preferred course. The contact workflow must be connected to a working email, CRM or WhatsApp number before the site goes live.
